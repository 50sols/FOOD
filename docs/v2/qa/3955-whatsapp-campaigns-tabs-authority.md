- The active workspace body is now emitted **only** by a server-selected Blade `@if` branch. The campaign builder remains the original separately authorized, five-step contextual create flow outside the page tab panels; its selected IDs, store selections, preview, launch_action, consent and draft state are never reconstructed by tab navigation.
